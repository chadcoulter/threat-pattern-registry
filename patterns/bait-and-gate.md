# Bait-and-Gate

## Definition

A predation pattern in which an attractive public-facing result, offer, listing, or opportunity draws a user into a gated interaction where signup, identity disclosure, payment, or another extractive action is required before the user can verify, inspect, report, or continue.

## Structural Signature

`Public Lure -> Engagement -> Gate -> Extraction -> Conversion`

## Common Indicators

- unusually attractive public offer or result
- verification or reporting unavailable without account creation
- identity or contact information requested before meaningful validation
- item, result, or offer becomes harder to inspect after engagement
- conversion pressure exceeds the value of the original interaction
- external search or AI systems amplify the lure

## Extraction Targets

- account creation
- email or phone number
- identity attributes
- device or behavioral telemetry
- payment information
- future marketing or targeting value

## Evidence Threshold

A match is stronger when the same resource can be shown to have materially different visibility or behavior before and after the gate, especially when the gate itself creates measurable commercial or data value for the operator.

## Related Patterns

- Trust Hijack
- Synthetic Inventory
- Identity Harvesting
- Search / AI Poisoning
- Protective Interception

## Pattern Composition

Bait-and-Gate commonly appears as one stage in a compound predation chain rather than as a standalone event.

# EFF - Microsoft Comparative Scrutiny

This file is the counterfactual/control-group workbench for `eff-microsoft-001`.

Its purpose is to test whether EFF's treatment of Microsoft after 2014 differs materially from its treatment of comparable companies engaging in materially comparable conduct.

## Required Comparison Dimensions

For each peer, capture:

- date and nature of relevant Terms or privacy-policy change
- whether the change expands platform access, moderation authority, content use, account control, data use, or unilateral amendment power
- whether EFF publicly commented
- form of EFF response: Deeplinks article, legal brief, campaign, scorecard, testimony, coalition letter, silence
- response intensity
- whether EFF demonstrably knew of the conduct
- dependency vectors linking EFF to that peer at the time
- time between conduct and response

## Counterfactual Threshold

The case may move from `POTENTIAL_BLIND_SPOT` toward `CORROBORATED_BLIND_SPOT` if materially comparable conduct repeatedly produces:

```text
Response(peer) >> Response(Microsoft)
```

while:

```text
Dependency(Microsoft) > Dependency(peer)
```

and the issue remains within EFF's active doctrine and mission.

## Control Requirements

Avoid false equivalence. Conduct must be materially comparable in scope, user impact, contractual authority, and timing before response intensity is compared.

# Contributing Research Upstream to PLOT4AI

Use this boundary when local research produces a finding that may belong in PLOT4AI.

## Contribute upstream when

The finding can be expressed as an atomic AI threat or elicitation question with:

- a clear threat condition;
- affected lifecycle phases or roles;
- an explanation;
- mitigation or recommendation guidance; and
- source provenance.

## Keep local when

The finding is primarily:

- a composed multi-stage attack pattern;
- an evidence-resolution state machine;
- a relationship or dependency topology;
- a case-specific allegation or investigation; or
- a registry-specific transform.

## Exchange rule

PLOT4AI remains authoritative for PLOT4AI cards. The Threat Pattern Registry remains authoritative for local canonical patterns. Mappings connect the two without erasing provenance or license boundaries.
